ITE Kottakkal: Where Skills Meet Opportunity

Located in the heart of Cholakkundu, our campus is more than just a training center—it is a community designed for growth. At ITE Kottakkal, we combine rigorous technical training in NCVT & KGCE-certified trades with a vibrant campus life that includes sports, arts, and a dedicated resource library. We provide a supportive, hands-on environment where students gain the practical expertise needed to excel in their careers. Whether you are aiming for industry roles or PSC-level opportunities, ITE Kottakkal is the professional bridge to your future.
